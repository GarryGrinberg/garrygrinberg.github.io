What a soulmate date is — and isn't

A little honesty before you read yours.

♥

It's not a compatibility score.

Soulmate dates don't rank how well two people get along. They point to the birth dates whose pattern naturally lines up with yours — a different thing from compatibility.

♥

It's a map, not a verdict.

Don't run your current partner's birthday down the list to "test" your relationship — that's not what these dates are for. They show where connection comes most naturally; someone not being on yours isn't a red flag, just a different kind of match.

♥

Fewer dates isn't a worse result.

The count varies from person to person — some charts surface many dates, others only a few. Rarer just means more specific, not less special.
